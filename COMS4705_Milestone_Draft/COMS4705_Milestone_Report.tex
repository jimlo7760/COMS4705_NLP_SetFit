\begin{abstract}
We study whether SetFit's pairwise representation adaptation improves fine-grained banking intent detection enough to justify its cost. At 8 and 32 examples per intent, we compare SetFit with frozen embeddings plus logistic regression (Frozen+LR) and supervised cross-entropy (CE) tuning on BANKING77. A preliminary dataset audit identifies duplicate and overlapping texts and creates disjoint evaluation splits. Preliminary findings concern dataset integrity; model scores are pending. We specify matched training, complete checkpoint-selection timing, and seed-wise analyses of intent confusions.
\end{abstract}

\section{Approach}
\textbf{Task and motivation.} BANKING77 is 77-way, single-label English classification of banking support requests \citep{casanueva2020efficient,polyai2020banking77}. SetFit learns from same/different-label sentence pairs \citep{tunstall2022setfit}, while frozen sentence encoders can already work well for intent detection \citep{ma2022sentence}. We ask whether adaptation beats a matched frozen encoder consistently across samples and at an acceptable cost, particularly for confusable labels \citep{zhang2025clear}.

\textbf{Matched methods.} All methods start from \texttt{all-MiniLM-L6-v2} (22.7M parameters; 384-dimensional, mean-pooled, normalized embeddings) \citep{minilm2026}. (1) \textbf{Frozen+LR} freezes the encoder and fits multinomial logistic regression (LR). (2) \textbf{CE tuning} jointly trains the encoder and a 77-way linear head with cross-entropy. (3) \textbf{SetFit} updates the encoder with balanced positive/negative sentence pairs, re-embeds the selected labeled texts, then fits the \emph{same} LR configuration as Frozen+LR. Its pair objective is
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{pair}}=\frac{1}{B}\sum_{i=1}^{B}
  \bigl[\cos(f_{\theta}(x_i),f_{\theta}(x'_i))-z_i\bigr]^2,
  \qquad z_i=\mathbf{1}[y_i=y'_i].
\end{equation}
For each anchor, we draw four distinct positive and four distinct negative partners from the selected training set (4,928/19,712 pairs). Both LR models use \texttt{lbfgs}, $C=1$, intercept, and 2,000 maximum iterations; CE is compared at the pipeline level. Our planned custom PyTorch loops and pair sampler use published SetFit code as reference, alongside pretrained weights and standard LR/metric implementations \citep{setfit2026}.

\textbf{Implementation status.} The data audit and study protocol are documented. The next targets are to verify all three model pipelines, checkpoint selection, and timing instrumentation before reporting training results.

\section{Experiments}
\textbf{Data and preliminary findings.} The proposal's exact-text audit (after trimming whitespace) found four redundant training rows and six cross-split texts. Removing them from training leaves 9,993 rows; the 3,080 official test rows remain untouched. Stratified validation (seed 2026) retains 965 examples, leaving 9,028 training candidates and at least 32 per intent (Table~\ref{tab:data}). These are \emph{data-audit findings}, not model scores.

\textbf{Protocol and metrics.} Nested per-class $k\in\{8,32\}$ samples use seed 0 for CE/SetFit learning-rate tuning ($10^{-5}$ vs. $3\times10^{-5}$), then seeds 11, 22, and 33 for paired final runs. All methods share each sampled training subset. We use dynamic padding, 128-token truncation (frequency logged), AdamW for encoder updates (weight decay 0.01), batch size 32, gradient clipping 1.0, 10\% warmup/linear decay, and FP16 where available. The 8/32-shot caps are 300/1,000 updates; checkpoints are selected by validation macro-F1 every 50/100 updates, favoring the earlier in a tie. Test macro-F1 (mean of 77 class F1s; undefined $=0$) is primary; accuracy and class recall are secondary. Test labels never inform model selection. Across final seeds, report means, sample SDs, and paired SetFit-minus-baseline differences. Because sampling and optimization both vary with seed, interpret SD as run-to-run variability, not a pure sample effect.

\textbf{Cost accounting and preliminary model status.} Following the TA's feedback, SetFit training time will include pairing, encoder updates, re-embedding, \emph{every checkpoint-time LR fit}, and validation; test inference and setup overhead are recorded separately. The schedule implies 6/10 LR fits per 8/32-shot run: 32 across tuning and 48 across final SetFit runs, plus any final refits. We will log component/total times and peak GPU memory with consistent hardware boundaries. Preliminary model scores and times are not yet available; Table~\ref{tab:models} marks them pending.
